# Cellar House — Studio Strategy

1. **Customer:** Wine enthusiasts and gift hosts who want discovery — vintage context, pairing intelligence, and cellar atmosphere without generic vineyard stock tropes.
2. **5-second feel:** Low-lit cellar library — burgundy film hero, wax-seal accents, “I found something special behind this door.”
3. **Signature:** Vintage Timeline (scrollable release years with peak windows) + food×wine Pairing Matrix on homepage; full tasting desk at `/tasting`.
4. **Type & color:** Libre Baskerville display + Karla body; dark `#1A0F14` cellar ground, `#7F1D1D` primary, gold accent — no purple AI gradients.
5. **Journey:** Cellar film enter → 3D rack bottle → timeline → pairing matrix → sommelier score reviews → shop → PDP aging curve + tasting chips → CELLAR12 case checkout (6+ bottles).
6. **Motion:** Slow hero rise, soft CTA pulse, marquee optional on shop; reduced-motion respects prefers-reduced-motion on orbit and video.
7. **3D usefulness:** Scene3D burgundy bottle on rack — drag to inspect foil and glass; supports pairing story, not a widget bolt-on.
8. **Conversion hooks:** Case discount banner, AI sommelier canned pairings, club loyalty line, newsletter, wax-seal flavor chips, warehouse tasting bar in footer.
